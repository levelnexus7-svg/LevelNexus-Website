Services

Material, accreditation support and the documentation in between — for Skills Development Providers who need to be delivering, not drafting.

Service 01

QCTO-aligned learning material

A complete, curriculum-mapped material set for the occupational qualification you are registered against — supplied editable so it goes out under your brand.

  • Learner guides and facilitator guides per knowledge module
  • Practical skills workbooks and simulated workplace activities
  • Work experience logbooks and workplace mentor packs
  • Formative and summative assessment instruments with memoranda
  • Alignment matrix mapping content to the QCTO curriculum document
  • Drawn from 155 occupational qualifications already on file
Service 02

Accreditation support for providers

Most applications stall on documentation, not capability. We work through the file with you until it is defensible.

  • Gap analysis against QCTO and SETA accreditation criteria
  • Quality Management System documentation and policy set
  • Assessor and moderator appointment records and matrices
  • Site and facility readiness against programme requirements
  • Evidence file assembly and pre-submission review
  • Support through evaluator queries and site visits
Service 03

Skills programmes and legacy material

Short, fundable programmes that fill a training calendar and give learners a credential without a full qualification cycle.

  • 81 SP-coded skills programmes across every major sector
  • MQA mining programmes including underground hard rock
  • Legacy unit-standard-based material still in demand by employers
  • First aid, OHS representative and evacuation programme sets
  • Foundational learning and end user computing material
  • Ideal for discretionary grant and SETA-funded intakes
Service 04

Customisation, branding and development

When the register does not have it, or when what you have needs to become yours.

  • Full rebrand to your identity — cover, colour, typography, footer
  • Content adaptation to your delivery model and learner profile
  • New material development against a curriculum document
  • Translation and plain-language simplification
  • Print-ready artwork and bulk print coordination
  • Version control and update service as curricula are revised
