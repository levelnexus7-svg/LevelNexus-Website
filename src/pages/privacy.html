Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information Level Nexus collects through this website, why, and what your rights are under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who is responsible

Level Nexus, based in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, is the responsible party for information you send us through this website. You can contact us at levelnexus@outlook.com or on +27 84 762 4831.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form we collect the details you type in: your name, organisation (optional), email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), what you need, and your message. When you use the shortlist on the Materials Register, the programmes you choose are kept in your own browser so they are there when you return; they reach us only if you send them.

Why we collect it

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To keep an ordinary business record of the conversation.

We do not send newsletters, build mailing lists or sell your information.

Who else sees it

If you choose to send an enquiry or shortlist through WhatsApp, the message travels through WhatsApp under its own terms. The home page can show our latest Facebook post; loading it connects your browser to Facebook. Other than these services you choose to use, we do not share your information with third parties unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as needed to answer them and to keep normal business records, and then delete them.

Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may object to us using it. Email us and we will respond. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Regulator at inforeg.org.za.
