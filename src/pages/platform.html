Each role gets its own view

Administrators, assessors, moderators and learners see the screens they need, and nothing else. Screens below use demonstration data.

Run the whole provider from one dashboard

Administrators see everything, including users and settings. The dashboard flags what needs attention: overdue enrolments, modules a moderator has not confirmed, and accreditation dates running out.

  • Add qualifications with exact module codes, credits and NQF levels, and enrol learners one at a time or by CSV import
  • Run the QCTO quarter pack and other reports, exported as CSV or Excel
  • Back up on demand and keep staff logins to the least role that does the job
Learners

Mark a whole cohort on one grid

Assessors work from a queue of modules awaiting assessment, oldest first, and record each attempt with its date, outcome and note.

  • Tick cells on the marking grid and record a result for the whole group; skipped cells are listed afterwards
  • Every attempt is kept: earlier attempts are never changed
  • Log appeals, with the date and outcome, against the module they concern
Marking grid

Confirm second-person moderation

Moderators see a queue of modules awaiting moderation. They can read the assessor's notes and every attempt, then confirm the module or send it back for review.

  • The system will not let anyone confirm their own assessment
  • Anything not confirmed stays on the dashboard's needs-attention list until it is reviewed again
  • A learner counts as ready for EISA only when every module is assessed competent and internally moderated
Reports

A focused home for each learner

Learners see their own results by module, their key dates and their lessons, and nothing that belongs to anyone else.

  • Study lessons, including documents, video and Google Drive links, and tick them off as a personal record
  • See every attempt on every module, and download their own data
  • Print the provider's certificate of completion once it is issued

Ready for a verification visit

A QCTO or SETA verification visit asks a provider to show its records. Nexus Learn helps you produce them from live data instead of from memory.

Nexus Learn supports an accreditation file; it does not grant accreditation or replace evidence you keep elsewhere, such as signed agreements and learner portfolios. This list is a starting point, not the QCTO's own checklist, so check it against the letter announcing each visit.

Built to protect learner information

Learner records hold identity numbers, disability status and demographic categories. That is special personal information under POPIA, so the protections are built in.

Rules enforced on the server

Access is controlled by database security rules, not just hidden in the screens. The release notes record 258 of 258 security-rule tests passing on 14 October 2026.

Assessor notes stay with staff

Evidence notes and appeal notes are held separately, so a learner who opens their own record cannot read them.

POPIA tools

A consent tick and date on the learner form, a one-click data download, anonymise, a retention review report and Information Officer settings.

Automatic sign-out

Idle sessions are signed out after 20 minutes, with a warning first, and weak passwords are refused.

Backups

Automatic daily backup snapshots, a one-click backup you can download, and a banner that warns when a backup is overdue.
