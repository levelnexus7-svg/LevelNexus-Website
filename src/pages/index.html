Material that survives the site visit

When an evaluator opens your file, they check the material against the curriculum document. These sets are built for that check.

  • Mapped to the curriculum document. Knowledge, practical and work experience modules are referenced against the QCTO curriculum, not loosely themed around the job title.
  • Assessment-ready. Formative and summative instruments, memoranda and logbooks arrive as a set, so your assessor and moderator aren't building them from scratch.
  • Branded as yours. Delivered in editable format and styled to your identity. Your learners see your organisation, not ours.
  • Ready in days. Most of the 252 programmes in the register are already developed and checked, so there is no writing queue to wait in.
